FAQ

Is there a dress code?

Casual is fine — slippers and full training wear may be refused.

Age limit?

19+ (Korean age) with valid ID required. Passport or driver's license accepted.

Can I walk in without booking?

Walk-ins welcome, but we recommend guestlist or a table on Fri–Sat.

Is parking available?

No private parking — use public lots near Seomyeon Station. Taxi or metro recommended!

Can I smoke?

There is a smoking room on the 3rd floor.

Group / private hire?

DM us on Instagram @groovenspot or give us a call.

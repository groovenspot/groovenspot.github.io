FAQ

FAQ

Busan Nightlife Guide: Seomyeon Clubs →
Is there an entry fee / cover charge?

No — entry is free. Order drinks at the bar or book a table / bottle set.

Can foreigners and tourists get in?

Yes, foreigners are very welcome. Please bring your physical passport — photos or copies are not accepted.

What are the opening hours?

Open 365 nights a year: Sun–Thu 9:30 PM – 6 AM, Fri, Sat and nights before public holidays 9 PM – 7 AM.

What kind of music do you play?

2F GROOVE plays hip-hop, R&B and all kinds of music; 3F SPOT is EDM. Resident and guest DJs play every night.

Where can I see tonight's DJ timetable?

Every afternoon the night's timetable is posted on Instagram @groovenspot (stories) and on the events page of this website.

How do I book a table?

Use the Reserve page, DM us on Instagram or WhatsApp (@groovenspot), or call +82-507-1301-5970 with your date and party size.

Is there a dress code?

Yes. Overly casual wear, training wear (tracksuits) and slippers or sports sandals may be refused at the door.

Age limit?

19+ (Korean age) with valid ID required. Passport or driver's license accepted.

Can I walk in without booking?

Walk-ins welcome, but we recommend guestlist or a table on Fri–Sat.

Is parking available?

No private parking — use public lots near Seomyeon Station. Taxi or metro recommended!

Can I smoke?

There is a smoking room on the 3rd floor.

Group / private hire?

DM us on Instagram @groovenspot or give us a call.

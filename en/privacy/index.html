Privacy policy

Groove & Spot ("the club") respects the privacy of everyone using this website and our guests at the club. There are no accounts or payments here; we only handle the minimum information the features below and our bottle keep and guest service need.

Effective: 2026-11-21

2. Third-party services and overseas transfer

  • Google LLC (USA) — stores booking requests (Google Sheets / Apps Script). Only the club owner and assigned staff can see them; auto-deleted 1 year after the visit date.
  • Google LLC (USA) — Google Analytics 4. Google privacy policy. Opt out with the browser add-on.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) — runs the staff server (stores bottle keep, My page and guest service data). Data transferred: the bottle keep, My page and guest service items above, plus access logs (e.g. IP) · Location: set to Cloudflare's Northeast Asia (Korea / Japan) data centres · When and how: sent over encrypted HTTPS each time staff enter data or you open your link · Kept: for the retention periods above. Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Google LLC (USA) — Google Sheets view copy and daily Google Drive backup of the staff server. Data transferred: same as above (no photo files) · When and how: over HTTPS within minutes of a change (sheet copy) and every morning (backup file) · Kept: the sheet copy follows the server; backup files are deleted automatically 30 days after they are made.
  • ntfy.sh (Germany) — relays song requests to the DJ screen; deleted after 12 hours.
  • EMQX / HiveMQ public relays — virtual club presence and chat; nothing stored. These are public servers, so never post personal information in chat.
  • GitHub Pages (USA) — hosting; server logs (e.g. IP) may be kept.
  • Instagram, YouTube, Google Maps — embedded photos, videos and maps under their own policies.

If you do not want your bottle keep, My page or guest service data transferred abroad (Cloudflare, Google), tell your staff member or contact us below. In that case the keep link and My page cannot be used.

3. Cookies

We use Google Analytics cookies for statistics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser; the site still works.

4. Gallery photos

The gallery shows photos from our parties. To have a photo of you removed, DM @groovenspot on Instagram with the album date. We remove it promptly.

5. Deletion

When a retention period ends or you ask us to delete your data, we delete it from the staff server and the sheet copy without delay. Anything left in backup files is deleted automatically once the 30-day backup period passes.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your information via the contact below. For bottle keep and My page data, use 'Request deletion' on My page, ask your staff member or contact us below; you can withdraw offer consent at any time. While bottles remain in your keep, the minimum needed to store and return them (name or nickname, drinks, bottle count) may be kept. People under 19 may not use this site or the club.

7. Security

The site is HTTPS-only. Booking requests are stored only in the club's private Google Sheet, visible to the owner and assigned staff. Admin features require a separate key. Bottle keep, My page and guest service data can only be seen by approved staff after logging in; each staff member sees only their own guests, and only the owner and designated managers see everything. Your link uses a long, hard-to-guess key; if it reaches someone else we can issue a new link and the old one stops working at once. My page opens only when both the link and the last 4 digits of your phone match.

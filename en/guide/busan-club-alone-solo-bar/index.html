Going clubbing alone in Busan: a solo night out in Seomyeon

Traveling solo, or just nobody free tonight? Going to a club alone in Busan is no big deal — Groove & Spot has a solo bar on 2F that's easy to settle into when you come on your own. Here's what to know before you go.

Updated: 2026-11-19

Quick answers

  • Yes, you can go alone — 2F has a solo bar with 32 seats (H1–H32)
  • Entry is free and walk-ins are welcome (guest list recommended on Fridays and Saturdays)
  • 19+ by Korean year-age (born in 2007 or earlier); foreigners need an original passport
  • Bar menu: cocktails ₩6,000–₩10,000 · shots from ₩6,000 · bottled beer ₩8,000–₩10,000
  • Hours: Sun–Thu 9:30 PM – 6 AM · Fri, Sat & nights before holidays 9 PM – 7 AM

The solo bar on 2F (H seats)

On 2F GROOVE there's a solo bar that's easy if you come alone — seats H1 to H32, 32 in total. It's a good spot to sit with a drink while the hip-hop and R&B plays.

2F also has darts, so there's something to do between songs. You can see where everything is on the table layout on our floors page.

Want an H seat held for you?

Walk-ins are fine. On Fridays, Saturdays and guest DJ nights, though, we recommend booking ahead: choose "Solo bar H" as the booking type on our booking page, message us on WhatsApp or Instagram (@groovenspot), or call +82-507-1301-5970.

At the door: just bring your ID

  • No cover charge.
  • 19+ by Korean year-age — this year, anyone born in 2007 or earlier.
  • Physical ID only: foreigners need their original passport. Photos on your phone and copies are not accepted.
  • Tracksuits, slippers, sports sandals and overly casual outfits may be refused.

Details in our dress code & ID guide.

Ordering at the bar

On your own, ordering one drink at a time from the bar menu is the easy way to go. Prices are in Korean won with VAT included and may change on site.

  • Cocktails: vodka tonic, gin tonic, rum & coke ₩6,000 · Jack & coke, Jameson ginger ale ₩8,000 · Long Island iced tea ₩10,000
  • Shots & bombs: Jäger shot, Jäger bomb, vodka shot ₩6,000 · Jack shot ₩7,000
  • Bottled beer: Hoegaarden ₩8,000 · Budweiser, Corona ₩9,000 · Stella, Guinness ₩10,000
  • Non-alcoholic: grapefruit juice ₩7,000 · Cinderella ₩8,000 · Cass Zero ₩9,000

Want a cocktail, shooter or bomb that isn't listed? Ask the staff, and hand your order slip to the bartender. Full prices are on our menu page.

Two floors, one free entry

You can move between both floors at no extra cost — they're connected by stairs inside the building.

  • 2F GROOVE: hip-hop & R&B · solo bar (H seats) · darts
  • 3F SPOT: EDM on a big-room sound system · L seats facing the main stage · photo zone · smoking room

Smoking is only allowed in the 3F smoking room. Not sure which floor is for you? Read hip-hop (2F) vs EDM (3F).

When to go

Doors open at 9:30 PM Sunday to Thursday and 9 PM on Fridays, Saturdays and nights before holidays. Every afternoon, the night's DJ timetable goes up on our events page and Instagram stories (@groovenspot) — pick a DJ you like and time your arrival to their set.

Feeling at ease on your own

  • Respect each other on the floor. If anything feels wrong, tell our staff right away.
  • Lost something? Items found at the club are posted by date, with photos, on our Lost & Found page. If you see yours, send us the date and the photo by phone or Instagram DM — we return it at the club after checking your ID.
  • The metro stops around midnight, so if you're out late, plan on a taxi (Kakao T) back to your hotel. Getting here: how to get to Seomyeon from Busan Station, Haeundae and Gimhae Airport.

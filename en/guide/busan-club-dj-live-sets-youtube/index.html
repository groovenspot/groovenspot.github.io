Hear the DJs before you go: Groove & Spot live sets on YouTube

The quickest way to know if a club is your kind of night is to listen to it. We post live sets from the DJs at Groove & Spot in Seomyeon, Busan on our YouTube channel GROOVE CLUB BUSAN (@GROOVEBUSAN) as GROOVE SESSIONS. Here's who plays which floor, and where to start.

Updated: 2026-11-19

Quick facts

  • GROOVE SESSIONS: our series of DJ live set videos — 37 sets so far
  • Channel: GROOVE CLUB BUSAN (@GROOVEBUSAN) on YouTube
  • Length: hip-hop sets run about an hour, most EDM sets about 40 minutes
  • Prefer a music app? Listen on YouTube Music, or hear our official mixes on SoundCloud
  • The newest sets also appear in the Live sets section of our home page

DJs who play both floors

On our timetable from August to November 2026, these DJs often play both 2F and 3F on the same night — a good place to start if you like a bit of everything.

  • TARO · GROOVE SESSIONS 037 (39:09) — from hard-hitting bass house to late-night melodic techno
  • ROAD · GROOVE SESSIONS 027 (41:08) — melodic drum & bass and bounce
  • UZI · GROOVE SESSIONS 021 (43:12) — tech house and afro for late nights in the studio
  • MUTE · GROOVE SESSIONS 023 (38:20) — tech house and breakbeat for a night drive

MUMU is another regular on both floors' timetables.

Regulars on 3F SPOT (EDM)

  • EZZY · GROOVE SESSIONS 033 (41:26) — melodic bounce and tech house, a Friday-night EDM party mix

  • DIA · GROOVE SESSIONS 015 (39:57) — addictive Vinahouse melodic bounce

  • CHOPPER · GROOVE SESSIONS 026 (34:03) — rave, hard dance and speed house
  • YUKA · GROOVE SESSIONS 012 (30:24) — tech house and breakbeat, a gym session in a mix
  • GIO · GROOVE SESSIONS 019 (39:25) — speed house and bounce to hit a new PR
  • POTAITO · GROOVE SESSIONS 004 (23:14) — melodic bounce for heavy lifting

For work, the commute and night drives

A DJ mix keeps going without gaps for 30–40 minutes, so you can leave one running while you work or drive. Start with the UZI (late-night studio) and JOHNYRIGHTHERE (ride home) sets above, then try:

  • GG · GROOVE SESSIONS 006 (35:34) — the ultimate driving-mood melodic house bounce
  • BUCKY · GROOVE SESSIONS 025 (39:21) — Korea bounce and melodic house for a late-night drive
  • AUREDE · GROOVE SESSIONS 002 (39:05) — an emotional melodic bounce driving mix
  • JUNTARO · GROOVE SESSIONS 034 (41:01) — speed house, future house and tech house, foot on the gas

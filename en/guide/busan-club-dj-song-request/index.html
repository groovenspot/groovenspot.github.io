How to request a song from the DJ at a Busan club

Want to hear a certain track tonight? You don't have to shout over the music at the DJ booth. At Groove & Spot, requests sent from your phone go straight to the DJ's screen. Here's exactly how our request page works.

Updated: 2026-11-19

Quick answers

  • Where: our song request page — it opens in your phone's browser, no app or login
  • Required: just the song title (artist, nickname and note are optional)
  • Floor: 2F hip-hop or 3F EDM
  • Your request goes straight to the DJ's screen
  • One request per minute
  • Not every track gets played — if it fits the vibe, the DJ plays it

Where to find the request page

  • Requests in the top menu
  • 🎵 Requests in the bar at the bottom of your phone screen
  • The Request a song button in the TONIGHT box on our home page — during opening hours it also shows who's playing now and who's next
  • The Request a song button for each floor on our floors page — it opens with that floor already selected

Step 1: Find the song

Type at least two characters of a title or artist into the Song title box and matching tracks appear below. Tap one and the title and artist fill in automatically.

  • K-pop and Korean hip-hop artists can be found by their English or Korean names.
  • Can't find it, or search isn't working? Just type the title and send it as is.

Join a song someone already requested

If people have sent requests in the last 12 hours, up to six of them appear under the box as 🔥 Requested tonight. Tap one to fill it in and request it too.

Step 2: Pick your floor and send

  • Which floor? — 2F · HIPHOP or 3F · EDM. Pick the floor you're on; your request reaches the DJ with the floor attached.
  • Nickname (optional, up to 20 characters) — your phone remembers it for next time.
  • Note to the DJ (optional, up to 60 characters)
  • Tap Send request. When you see "Request sent! 🎵", you're done.

Good to know

  • After one request, you can send the next one a minute later. Tap too early and you'll see how many seconds are left.
  • Not every track can be played. If it fits the vibe, the DJ will play it.
  • Don't include phone numbers or other personal info.
  • Requests (song, artist, floor, nickname, note) are deleted automatically by the relay service after 12 hours. See our privacy policy.
  • If sending fails, wait a moment and try again.

Tips to boost your chances

  • Match the floor: 2F plays hip-hop and R&B, 3F plays EDM. See hip-hop (2F) vs EDM (3F).
  • Check who's playing first. The TONIGHT box on our home page and the floors page show each floor's timetable.
  • Curious about a DJ's style? Hear their GROOVE SESSIONS sets on YouTube first — DJ live sets guide.

First time at Groove & Spot?

Entry is free. You need to be 19+ by Korean year-age (born in 2007 or earlier) and bring a physical ID — foreigners need an original passport. Doors open at 9:30 PM Sunday to Thursday and 9 PM on Fridays, Saturdays and nights before holidays. More in our dress code & ID guide and Busan nightlife guide.

Busan nightlife guide: clubbing in Seomyeon

Seomyeon is the center of Busan nightlife. Here is everything you need before your first night out, from the team at Groove & Spot.

Entry
Free — no cover charge
Hours
Every night · Sun–Thu 9:30 PM – 6 AM · Fri, Sat & nights before holidays 9 PM – 7 AM
Location
Seomyeon Station Exit 3, 3 min walk (52 Jungang-daero 680beon-ga-gil, 2F–3F)
Age
19+ by Korean year-age — anyone born in 2007 or earlier
ID
Physical ID required · foreigners: original passport
Music
2F hip-hop & R&B · 3F EDM

Getting there

Take Busan Metro Line 1 or 2 to Seomyeon Station and leave by Exit 3. The club is a 3-minute walk.

  • From Busan Station: about 15 min on Line 1
  • From Haeundae: about 35 min on Line 2, or about 25 min by taxi
  • From Gimhae Airport: light rail to Sasang, then Line 2 — about 45 min

For taxis, show the driver the Korean address: 부산 부산진구 중앙대로680번가길 52. The metro stops around midnight, so plan to take a taxi home.

Entry rules

  • Entry is free. Order drinks at the bar or book a table / bottle set.
  • Korea counts age by calendar year: you can enter if you were born in 2007 or earlier.
  • Bring your physical passport. Photos and copies are not accepted.
  • Casual dress is fine. Slippers and full training wear may be refused.

Two floors, two vibes

2F GROOVE — hip-hop and R&B. A solo bar (H seats) if you come alone, stage seats and tables for groups, plus darts.

3F SPOT — big-room EDM with L seats facing the main stage, a photo zone and a smoking room.

Every afternoon the night's DJ timetable goes up on Instagram @groovenspot (stories) and on our events page.

Drinks and tables

Prices are in Korean won (₩) with VAT included. On Fridays, Saturdays and guest DJ nights we recommend booking a table — use the booking page, Instagram DM, or call +82-507-1301-5970.

Good to know

  • No private parking — take the metro or a taxi.
  • Smoking only in the 3F smoking room.
  • If anything feels wrong, tell our staff right away.
